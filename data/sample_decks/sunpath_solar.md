# SunPath Energy Ltd — Series A Pitch

*Fictional sample company created for the Impact Vision demo. Any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.*

## The problem

Around 25 million people in Kenya still light their homes with kerosene or have no electricity at all. Kerosene is expensive, causes indoor air pollution and burns, and keeps children from studying after dark. Grid extension to remote rural counties is slow and costly.

## Our solution

SunPath Energy Ltd sells pay-as-you-go solar home systems to off-grid households in rural Kenya. Customers pay a small deposit and then daily instalments by mobile money; after 18 months they own the system outright. Each kit powers four lights, phone charging and a radio, and can be upgraded to a TV.

## Traction

We have connected 42,000 households across 14 counties, replacing kerosene lamps and reducing household energy spend by 30%. Our systems avoided an estimated 20,000 tCO2e in 2024. Repayment rates stay above 92% and we employ 310 staff, of whom 41% are women.

## Who we serve

62% of our customers live below the national poverty line according to the Poverty Probability Index, and 71% had no electricity before buying from us. We track customer repayment and satisfaction monthly through call-centre surveys and a grievance line.

## Evidence

A 2025 study by an independent evaluator compared customers with a matched control group and found 18% higher evening study hours for children and lower reported respiratory symptoms. Our carbon methodology is verified by a third-party auditor under Gold Standard rules.

## Risks we manage

Over-indebtedness: we cap instalments at 10% of estimated household income and offer payment holidays after crop failures. E-waste: we take back and recycle batteries at end of life through a certified partner. Product safety: all kits meet the Lighting Global quality standard.

## Targets

Over the next three years we target 150,000 connected households and 80,000 tCO2e avoided per year, and we report against IRIS+, SDG 7/1/13 and the GOGLA impact metrics.
