# BrightPath Finance — Growth Round Investor Memo

*Fictional sample company created for the Impact Vision demo. Any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.*

## Overview

BrightPath Finance is a digital microfinance platform providing affordable credit and savings products to smallholder farmers and women-led micro-enterprises in Sub-Saharan Africa. Loans are disbursed and repaid by mobile money, with agronomy advice bundled for farmer clients.

## Reach

We serve 45,000 clients (PI4060: 45,000), 68% of them women. Our client model is direct-to-consumer lending (OD8350). We have a published Client Protection Policy (OI4753) and run NPS surveys plus a grievance system (OI5049).

## Team & governance

We employ 180 permanent staff (OI8869: 180), including 85 women (OI6213: 85) and 12 female managers (OI1571: 12). Our wage equity ratio is 0.92 (OI1582). The board has 7 directors (OI1075: 7). We set 3 social and 2 environmental targets each year (OD4091) and publish an annual impact report (OI4732). Community advisory boards guide product design (OI4324).

## Performance

Revenue grew 32% last year (FP4761: 32%). Portfolio at risk over 30 days is 3.1%. Our own footprint is 120 tCO2e (OI1479: 120 tCO2e), of which 80 tCO2e are direct Scope 1 emissions (OI4112: 80 tCO2e).

## Risks we manage

Over-indebtedness is our main client risk: we check credit bureau data, cap loan sizes against cash flow and train loan officers on responsible lending. Data privacy: client data is encrypted and we comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act.

## Alignment

We contribute to SDGs 1, 5, 8 and 10 and report against IRIS+ and the Client Protection Principles.
