# Ubuzima Mutuelle Plus — Impact Fund Round

Ubuzima Plus is a community-based health insurance scheme in Rwanda that covers outpatient care and referrals for informal workers and smallholder farmers in Eastern Province, working with district hospitals and health centres.

## The problem
Out-of-pocket payments push families into debt or lead them to delay care. Informal workers often fall outside the national mutuelle top-up.

## Impact
- 148,000 members in 5 districts (2025); 64% in the lowest two Ubudehe wealth categories.
- Matched comparison study (propensity matching, 4,100 households, 2024): members used outpatient services 3.1 times per year vs 1.9 for non-members; catastrophic health spending 6% vs 14%.
- Member records cross-checked against facility claims; third-party audit firm verified enrolment, claims and the study dataset, report available in the data room.
- Renewal rate 88%.

## Limits
Matching cannot rule out that healthier or more motivated households join; we report this openly and are adding a staggered rollout.
