# Roshan Raahein Foundation Fund — Impact Note

Roshan Raahein funds secondary-school scholarships and transport stipends for girls from low-income families in rural Punjab and Sindh, Pakistan, through partnerships with 46 government schools.

## The problem
Only about 36% of girls in rural Sindh complete lower secondary school. Fees, distance and early marriage push girls out after grade 5.

## Results
- 3,150 girls enrolled in the programme since 2020.
- Enrolment of participating girls rose from 71% of eligible cohort (the year before joining) to 96% in year one.
- Completion of grade 10: 82% of the 2020 cohort versus 58% reported for their schools in the three years before the programme.
- Average maths scores in our own test rose from 38% to 51% over two years (before/after, tests set by our staff).

## Risks we manage
Girls are tracked by field officers; dropouts are followed up within 30 days. We do not currently track a non-participant group.
