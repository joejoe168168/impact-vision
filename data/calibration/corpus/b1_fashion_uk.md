# Nuvella London — Series A

Nuvella is a carbon-neutral, 100% sustainable fashion brand designing elevated essentials for conscious women in the United Kingdom and online worldwide.

## The planet comes first
Every Nuvella piece is made with love for the Earth. We are a fully sustainable brand, and all our collections are carbon neutral thanks to certified offsets purchased on every order. Our eco-conscious fabrics and guilt-free design let customers shop responsibly.

## Traction
- GBP 6.2m revenue in 2024, up 70%.
- 310,000 Instagram followers and a 4.8 star review average.
- Stocked in 40 boutiques across the UK.

## Our promise
Nuvella is on a journey to a greener future and a better world for generations to come.
