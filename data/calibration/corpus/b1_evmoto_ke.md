# Voltaboda Mobility — Series B

Voltaboda leases swappable-battery electric motorbikes to boda-boda taxi riders in Nairobi, Kisumu and Mombasa, Kenya, and runs the battery-swap network that powers them.

## The problem
Kenya has over 1.5 million petrol motorbike taxis. Riders spend about half their daily earnings on fuel and repairs, and the fleet emits heavily in dense cities.

## Results
- 6,800 e-motorbikes on the road; 41 swap stations; 14.2m paid kilometres in 2024.
- 7,900 tCO2e avoided in 2024 against a petrol-bike baseline measured from 300 instrumented petrol bikes; reviewed and verified by an accredited third-party verifier (ISO 14064-3), with Kenyan grid factor used for charging.
- Rider income: in an independent study (n=520 Voltaboda riders, n=480 petrol riders matched by town and tenure, two survey waves), Voltaboda riders' net monthly income was KES 6,900 higher (about 28%) after lease payments.
- Riders: 97% male, 3% female; we are piloting a women-rider financing track.

## Risks we manage
Battery end-of-life: take-back and recycling with a licensed recycler; 92% of packs returned in 2024.
