# FroidMer — Series A

FroidMer (fictional) rents solar-powered cold-storage units to artisanal fishers and fish traders at landing beaches in Saint-Louis, Joal and Mbour, Senegal.

## The problem
Fishers lose a large share of the catch to spoilage in the heat and sell at the lowest price when boats arrive together. Women fish processors bear most of the loss.

## Results
Spoilage is measured by weighing rejected fish at the beach before and after units were installed at each site, and from our own sales logs.

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Units installed | 14 | 31 |
| Fishers served | 1,100 | 2,600 |
| Tonnes stored | 820 | 2,150 |
| Spoilage, our sites | 24% | 11% |
| Avg. sale price uplift (reported) | 9% | 14% |

Baseline spoilage at neighbouring beaches without units was about 30% in a 2024 spot check of two sites. 38% of users are women.

## Risks we manage
Panel theft, electricity-free uptime in the rainy season, fisher dependence on our pricing.

## Ask
EUR 5m to add 80 units and fund an independent evaluation of income effects.
