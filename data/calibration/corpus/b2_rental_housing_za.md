# Umzi Homes — Series B

Umzi Homes builds and manages affordable rental apartments for households earning ZAR 6,000–18,000 a month in Johannesburg and Cape Town, South Africa, on infill sites near transport.

## The problem
Gap-market households are priced out of formal housing and often rent backyard shacks or overcrowded rooms far from work, spending 50%+ of income on rent and travel.

## Results
- 3,150 units occupied across 14 buildings, housing roughly 9,800 residents; 97% occupancy.
- Rents average ZAR 4,300 for a one-bed unit, 31% below the local market rate for comparable units. The comparison was verified by an independent auditor (sampled 240 listings; report available in the data room).
- Tenant survey by an external research firm (n=1,020, 2025): median commute fell from 74 to 38 minutes; 68% of tenants had moved from informal or overcrowded housing; rent as share of income averaged 27%.
- Eviction rate 1.4% a year; arrears payment plans offered before any notice.

## Risks we manage
Gentrification of surrounding areas (rent caps for 10 years in lease covenants), construction embodied carbon (green-star rated buildings).

## Ask
ZAR 350 million to add 2,500 units by 2029.
