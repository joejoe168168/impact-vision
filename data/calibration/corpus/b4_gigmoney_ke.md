# Pesa Hustle — Seed

Pesa Hustle (fictional) offers a mobile-money wallet with automatic micro-savings and short-term advances for boda-boda riders, delivery couriers and ride-hail drivers in Nairobi and Mombasa, Kenya.

## The problem
Gig workers earn irregular income, have no payslip and are excluded from formal credit and savings products.

## Traction
- 86,000 registered users, 31,000 monthly actives (Dec 2024).
- Average savings balance KES 2,400; total savings held KES 74m.
- In our 2024 in-app survey (1,100 responses), 64% said they save more than before joining; 41% said they avoided a loan-shark loan in the last quarter.
- 78% of users are men under 35; women are 12%.

## Impact thesis
Smoothing income volatility leads to better resilience and lower reliance on predatory credit. We do not yet have a comparison group.

## Risks
Regulatory change on digital lenders, default rates on advances (6% at 30 days), fraud.

## Ask
USD 3m to grow to 250,000 users and launch an insurance add-on.
