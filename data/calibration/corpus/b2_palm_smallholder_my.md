# Kebun Lestari Sdn Bhd — Series A

Kebun Lestari supports independent oil palm smallholders in Sabah and Sarawak, Malaysia, with agronomy training, group certification and a premium-paying offtake agreement to mills.

## The problem
Smallholders produce around 40% of Malaysian palm oil but lack access to certification and good practice, and are blamed for forest conversion.

## Progress
- 4,200 smallholders enrolled in our programme; average farm size 3.4 hectares.
- 38% of enrolled farmers are RSPO certified under a group scheme (the rest are in preparation).
- Farmers on our training track report yields about 15% higher (farmer self-report, not measured).
- We are a deforestation-free supply chain and our farms protect thousands of hectares of rainforest.
- Premium paid to certified farmers: MYR 40 per tonne of fresh fruit bunches.

## Risks we manage
Traceability to the plot is incomplete; we are rolling out GPS polygons. Grievance channel in place.

## Ask
USD 5 million to certify 8,000 more farmers and build a traceability system.
