# PaniPoint Bangladesh — Seed

PaniPoint installs prepaid "water ATMs" that dispense filtered, arsenic-treated drinking water for BDT 0.5 per litre in low-income districts of Khulna and Satkhira, Bangladesh, where groundwater is saline or arsenic-contaminated.

## The problem
Tens of millions of Bangladeshis drink water above the national arsenic limit, and coastal salinity makes tube wells unusable.

## Traction
| Metric | 2023 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| ATMs installed | 48 | 131 |
| Litres dispensed (m) | 9.1 | 27.6 |
| Registered card users | 21,400 | 63,000 |
| Share of users below BDT 300/day | 64% | 66% |
| Uptime | 91% | 94% |

- Water tested quarterly by a government-accredited lab; 100% of 2024 samples met Bangladesh drinking water standards for arsenic.
- A user survey (n=410, 2024) found 73% reported fewer stomach illnesses in their household since they began using the ATMs. No comparison group.
