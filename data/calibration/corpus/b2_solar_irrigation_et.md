# Abay Pumps PLC — Series A

Abay Pumps sells pay-as-you-go solar irrigation pumps to smallholder vegetable and teff farmers in the Amhara and Oromia regions of Ethiopia, financed through mobile-money instalments over 24 months.

## The problem
Most smallholders here farm one rain-fed season. Diesel pumps cost USD 600+ to buy and USD 1.2 per hour to run, so only larger farms irrigate.

## Results
- 6,400 pumps installed since 2021, reaching about 38,000 household members; 61% of customers farm under 1 hectare and 29% are female-headed.
- A randomised controlled trial run with Addis Ababa University (1,100 households, 18 months, pre-registered, published as a working paper) found irrigated plots yielded 41% more tomato and onion output than control households (p<0.01).
- Net farm income rose by USD 310 per household per year versus control (95% CI 190–430); the control group was offered pumps in year 3.
- Lender data: 93% of instalments repaid on time. Pump uptime audited by an independent engineer at 96%.

## Risks we manage
- Groundwater: well-level monitoring in 40 sites; abstraction caps in our contract.
- Displacement of hired labour: tracked in the RCT endline (no significant change in labour days).
- Pump batteries and panels returned under a take-back scheme.

## Ask
USD 6 million to reach 20,000 pumps by 2028 and fund a second RCT round on nutrition outcomes.
