# NozarDhaka Vision — Series B

NozarDhaka Vision (fictional) sells affordable prescription eyeglasses, from USD 3, through community optical shops and screening camps for garment workers and rural adults in Bangladesh.

## The problem
An estimated 30 million adults in Bangladesh need glasses but lack them; presbyopia alone cuts working productivity, especially in fine manual work.

## Results
- 410,000 pairs dispensed 2020-2024; 58% to women; 46% to people living on under USD 5 a day.
- Independent evaluation by a development-economics team (randomised offer of glasses vs delayed offer, 2,100 tea-garden and garment workers, 8-month follow-up): task output rose 11% for the treatment group vs comparison (95% CI 6-16%), and monthly earnings rose about 9%.
- Self-reported headaches and eye strain fell by a third in the treated group.
- Prices set so that 70% of cost is recovered from sales; subsidy is concentrated on the poorest buyers.

## Risks we manage
Sub-standard imports undercutting us, low replacement and follow-up rates (we track 6-month use: 78%), dependence on a few factory partners.

## Ask
USD 12m to reach 1.5 million pairs by 2028 and fund a longer-term follow-up of earnings.
