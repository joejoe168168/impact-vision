# VooVerde AgroTech — Seed

VooVerde offers drone spraying services to soybean and corn farmers in Mato Grosso, Brazil, delivering precision application of crop protection products.

## The problem
Conventional spraying wastes product and contaminates soil and waterways.

## Why we are different
Our smart drones apply only what the crop needs, so farmers use far less pesticide and protect the environment. Our customers tell us they are seeing big reductions and healthier fields.

## Traction
- Over 120 farms served and growing fast.
- Thousands of hectares sprayed.
- Positive feedback from farmers and cooperatives.
- Supports sustainable agriculture and several SDGs including Zero Hunger and Life on Land.

## Impact
We believe our model can cut pesticide use substantially and improve rural livelihoods across Brazil.
