# KhetiSuraksha — Seed

KhetiSuraksha sells weather-index microinsurance to smallholder farmers in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, India, bundled through farmer producer organisations and agri-input retailers.

## The problem
Erratic monsoons wipe out crops for millions of small farmers who have no safety net and fall back on moneylenders.

## Traction
- 212,000 policies sold in FY2024 (up from 96,000 in FY2023), covering 168,000 distinct farmers.
- INR 41 crore in claims paid in FY2024; median payout 11 days after the trigger.
- Claims ratio 68%; renewal rate 54%.
- 62% of policyholders hold less than 2 hectares.

## What we hear from farmers
In our annual phone survey, most farmers say they "feel more secure". We have not yet measured changes in income, borrowing or planting decisions.
