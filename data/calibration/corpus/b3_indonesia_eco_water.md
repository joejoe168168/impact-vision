# TerraPure Indonesia — Series B

TerraPure is an Indonesian bottled water brand sold in supermarkets and convenience stores across Java and Bali, marketed to health-conscious urban consumers.

## Our brand promise
TerraPure is 100% green. Our eco water is plastic-neutral: for every bottle sold, we offset its plastic through verified plastic credits, so your choice leaves no footprint. Natural, pure and kind to the planet.

## Traction
- Distribution in 14,000 retail outlets.
- Revenue growth of 85% year on year.
- Featured in lifestyle media as a leading sustainable beverage.

## Sustainability
- Our bottles are recyclable.
- We buy plastic credits from an international registry equal to our annual bottle volume.
- Our commitment to nature runs through everything we do.
