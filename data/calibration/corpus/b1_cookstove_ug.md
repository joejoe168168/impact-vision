# Mwoto Stoves Ltd — Series A

Mwoto Stoves manufactures and sells fuel-efficient rocket stoves on instalment plans to rural and peri-urban households in Mbale, Kapchorwa and Tororo districts, Uganda.

## The problem
Around 94% of Ugandan households cook on wood or charcoal. Smoke exposure drives childhood pneumonia, and families spend 15-25% of income on charcoal.

## Results
- 38,400 stoves sold since 2021; 31,200 households still using them at last survey (81% active use, verified by sensor-based stove-use monitors on a 600-stove sample).
- Independent evaluation by a Kampala university team (2024): 1,100 Mwoto households compared with 1,050 matched non-customer households in the same sub-counties, baseline and 18-month follow-up. Customers cut wood and charcoal use by 41% (controlled kitchen tests plus weighed fuel) and spent UGX 9,800 less per month on fuel versus comparison households.
- Personal exposure monitoring: PM2.5 down 52% in customer kitchens versus comparison.
- Carbon credits issued under Gold Standard TPDDTEC methodology with a third-party verifier; 64,000 tCO2e verified for 2023.

## Risks we manage
- Stacking with old stoves: we report only measured fuel savings, not assumed ones.
- Affordability: 24-month instalment plans; default rate 6%.
