# MamaPing Health — Series A

MamaPing runs a text-message and voice service that sends pregnant women in northern Nigeria timed antenatal reminders, danger-sign alerts and clinic referral codes, working through primary health centres in Kano and Kaduna states.

## The problem
Fewer than half of pregnant women in our states complete the four antenatal visits recommended by national guidelines. Late first visits mean missed anaemia, hypertension and infection screening.

## Results
- Cluster-randomised trial, 2023-2025: 40 clinics (20 MamaPing, 20 comparison), 6,820 women enrolled, baseline balanced on age, parity and distance to clinic.
- Four or more antenatal visits: 61% in MamaPing clinics vs 44% in comparison clinics (difference 17 points, 95% CI 11-23).
- Facility delivery: 58% vs 49%. Effects held after adjusting for clinic size and prior attendance.
- Evaluation designed and analysed by an independent university team; pre-registered protocol and full report are public. Attendance verified from clinic registers, not self-report.
- Current reach: 212,000 women registered across 310 clinics; 93% of enrolees below the national poverty line.

## Risks we manage
- Phone sharing: consent captured per woman, opt-out within one message; no data sold.
- Contribution: comparison arm tracks secular improvement, so we claim only the difference.
