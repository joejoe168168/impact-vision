# Azure Leaf Resorts — Growth Equity

Azure Leaf is a Thai chain of eco-luxury beachfront and jungle hotels in Phuket, Krabi and Chiang Mai, catering to affluent international travellers.

## Our green promise
We are a net zero hotel group. Every stay is carbon neutral, and our guests travel guilt-free in a pristine natural paradise. Our properties are planet-friendly, nature-inspired and committed to a greener tomorrow.

## How we get there
- We purchase carbon offsets for all of our operations and for guest flights.
- Bamboo straws, reef-friendly welcome gifts and a tree planted for each booking.
- Our brand is recognised as one of the most sustainable in Southeast Asia.

## Traction
- 9 hotels, 1,100 rooms, strong occupancy and award-winning spa.
- Sustainability champion in guest reviews.
