# Lastenrad Logistik GmbH — Series A

We run electric cargo-bike delivery from micro-hubs in the city centres of Hamburg, Cologne and Leipzig, Germany, replacing diesel van trips for parcel and grocery couriers.

## The problem
Urban delivery vans add congestion, NOx and CO2. Roughly 70% of inner-city parcels fit a cargo bike.

## Results

| Year | Deliveries | Bike km | tCO2e avoided |
|------|-----------:|--------:|--------------:|
| 2022 | 310,000 | 118,000 | 21 |
| 2023 | 740,000 | 262,000 | 49 |
| 2024 | 1,480,000 | 505,000 | 96 |

- Avoided emissions are calculated against a modelled diesel van baseline (UBA factors) and net of bike manufacturing and hub electricity (certified green tariff).
- Estimate prepared in-house; a TUeV review of the method is scheduled for 2025.
- 42 couriers employed on permanent contracts at above sector-minimum pay.

## Ask
EUR 8m to open 6 hubs and replace the remaining leased vans in our fleet.
