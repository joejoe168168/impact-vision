# Mitti Carbon Pvt Ltd — Series A

Mitti Carbon converts rice and cotton crop residue, which farmers would otherwise burn, into biochar at village-level pyrolysis units in Punjab and Maharashtra, India, and sells the biochar as a soil amendment and as carbon removal credits.

## The problem
Residue burning drives severe winter air pollution in north India and releases CO2 and black carbon.

## Results

| Year | Residue processed (t) | Biochar produced (t) | CO2e removed/avoided (t) | Farmers supplying |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| 2023 | 4,200 | 1,050 | 2,300 | 610 |
| 2024 | 11,800 | 2,950 | 6,900 | 1,640 |
| 2025 | 24,500 | 6,100 | 14,700 | 3,300 |

- CO2e figures follow a registry methodology for biochar; third-party validation is in progress, the 2025 figure is internally calculated.
- Farmers receive INR 1,800 per tonne of residue; 22% of supplying farmers are women-led holdings.
- Field trials on 120 plots reported 8–12% yield gains, with no control plots in the 2023 trials.
