# Lesotho Lane Readers — Series A

Leseli Reads (fictional) runs a 20-week structured reading programme for Grade R and Grade 1 teachers and learners in under-resourced public schools in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. We supply graded readers in isiXhosa and English, teacher coaching, and short weekly lesson scripts.

## The problem
Four in five South African Grade 4 children cannot read for meaning. The gap starts before Grade 1 and teachers lack structured materials in home languages.

## Results
Between 2022 and 2024 a cluster randomised trial, designed and analysed by a university education-research unit, assigned 60 schools (30 programme, 30 control) with 3,900 learners. Pre-registered on a public registry.
- Letter-sound knowledge: +0.31 standard deviations vs control at endline (p<0.01).
- Oral reading fluency: programme learners read 14 more words per minute than control.
- Effects held for girls and for the poorest quintile; attrition 6% in both arms.

## Reach
210 schools, 14,500 learners and 520 teachers in 2024. Cost per learner: USD 18.

## Risks we manage
Teacher turnover (we retrain within one term); provincial funding delays; effect fade-out, which we are testing with a Grade 3 follow-up in 2025.

## Ask
USD 4.5m to extend to two further provinces and fund the follow-up study.
