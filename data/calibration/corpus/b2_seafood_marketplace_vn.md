# BlueTide Market — Seed

BlueTide Market is an online marketplace in Vietnam connecting coastal fishers and aquaculture farms with restaurants and consumers in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, delivering premium, sustainably sourced seafood.

## Our promise
We are ocean positive. Every product on our platform is eco-friendly, responsibly sourced and kind to marine life. We believe in a future where the seas thrive, and our community of conscious consumers is leading the way.

## Why investors care
- A fast-growing market for sustainable seafood in Southeast Asia.
- A beautiful brand that stands for purity, nature and the ocean.
- Trusted by chefs who care about the planet.
- Aligned with SDG 14 (Life Below Water) and committed to protecting our oceans.

## Impact
By choosing BlueTide, customers help restore marine ecosystems and support coastal communities.
