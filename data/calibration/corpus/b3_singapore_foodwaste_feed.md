# LoopFeed Pte Ltd — Seed

LoopFeed collects food scraps from restaurants, food courts and hotels in Singapore, dehydrates them and turns them into animal feed pellets sold to local fish and poultry farms.

## The problem
Singapore generates over 800,000 tonnes of food waste a year, and only about one in five tonnes is recycled. Most of the rest is incinerated.

## Traction
- 112 partner restaurants and 6 food courts signed up.
- 1,940 tonnes of food waste diverted from incineration in 2024, based on weighbridge tickets at our plant.
- 410 tonnes of feed produced and sold to 9 farms, replacing part of imported soy and fishmeal.
- Estimated 1.6 tCO2e avoided per tonne diverted, taken from a published life-cycle study; we have not done our own LCA.

## Risks we manage
Contamination in food streams (we reject about 7% of loads) and feed safety: all batches tested by an NEA-approved lab.

## Ask
SGD 4m for a second dehydration line to reach 5,000 tonnes a year.
