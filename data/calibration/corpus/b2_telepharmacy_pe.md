# FarmaConecta — Pre-Seed

FarmaConecta is a telepharmacy platform linking remote communities in Peru with licensed pharmacists and delivery partners.

## Our vision
Healthcare should not depend on where you were born. We are transforming access to medicine and will touch millions of lives across Latin America. Our mission is to empower communities and build a healthier future.

## Traction
- Strong early momentum in the Andean and Amazon regions.
- Growing network of partner pharmacies and happy customers.
- Positive feedback from users and local health workers.

## Impact
We believe our model will improve health outcomes at scale and reduce inequality. We are aligned with SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) and SDG 10.
