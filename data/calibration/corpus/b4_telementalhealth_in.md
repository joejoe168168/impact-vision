# MannMitra — Seed

MannMitra (fictional) is a mobile app providing chat-based counselling and wellness content to college students across India, sold to universities and coaching institutes.

## The problem
Student mental health is a crisis in India and there is one psychiatrist per 100,000 people. We are changing that.

## Traction
- 1.2 million app downloads.
- "Over 3 million lives touched" through content, webinars and campus events.
- 140 campus partners.
- Featured in national press; winner of a startup award in 2023.

## Impact
Students tell us MannMitra makes a real difference. Our mission is to make India mentally healthier, one student at a time. We plan to measure outcomes as we scale.

## Team
Two founders with a psychology and engineering background; a clinical advisory board of three.

## Ask
USD 2m to build AI features and expand to 500 campuses.
