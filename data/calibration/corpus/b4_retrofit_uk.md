# WarmHome Works — Series B

WarmHome Works (fictional) delivers whole-house energy retrofits (insulation, heat pumps, ventilation) for low-income tenants in social and private rented homes across the North of England, United Kingdom, funded by housing associations and government schemes.

## The problem
2.9 million English households are in fuel poverty; poorly insulated rented homes are the main driver, and tenants cannot pay for upgrades.

## Impact
- 6,200 homes retrofitted 2021-2024. 71% of households were fuel poor under the official low-income-low-energy-efficiency definition.
- Smart-meter data for 1,480 homes, compared 12 months before and after: median gas use down 38%, electricity flat.
- Median annual bill saving GBP 540 after accounting for weather; 19% of treated households moved out of fuel poverty.
- Savings verified by an accredited retrofit assessor (PAS 2035) who audits a 10% sample each year; report is published.
- Reported indoor-temperature complaints fell by half in a tenant survey (n=900).

## Risks we manage
Performance gap of heat pumps, rebound effect (we report net of an estimated 12% comfort take-back), installer capacity.

## Ask
GBP 25m to scale to 20,000 homes and fund a matched-comparison study with a university.
