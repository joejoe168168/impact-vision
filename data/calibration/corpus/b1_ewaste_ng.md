# GreenLoop Africa — Pre-Seed

GreenLoop Africa is revolutionising electronic waste in Nigeria. We collect and refurbish used laptops and phones in Lagos and sell them at affordable prices.

## The problem
Nigeria is drowning in e-waste, and toxic dumping harms communities and the environment. Millions lack access to technology.

## Our impact
- We are transforming the circular economy in West Africa.
- Thousands of devices diverted from landfill.
- We create green jobs and bridge the digital divide for underserved youth.
- Our refurbishing process is safe and environmentally friendly.

## Traction
Operating since 2022 with a small team in Ikeja. A pilot with a bank recovered 400 laptops.

## Ask
USD 600k to scale across Nigeria and then all of Africa.
