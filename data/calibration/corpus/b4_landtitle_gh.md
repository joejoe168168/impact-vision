# TitleChain Ghana — Seed

TitleChain (fictional) is a digital land-titling platform that helps smallholder farmers and peri-urban families in Ashanti and Bono regions, Ghana, register and record their land parcels using GPS mapping, chief and neighbour witness and a blockchain-backed registry integrated with the Lands Commission.

## The problem
Most land in Ghana is held under customary tenure without documents, leading to disputes, low investment and exclusion from credit.

## Traction
- 38,000 parcels registered since 2022; 55% under joint or women-named ownership.
- 12 district partnerships and 90 trained field mappers.
- Average registration time 9 days vs several months through the formal route.
- Revenue from registration fees (GHS 80 per parcel) and a bank partnership for lending.

## Impact
Secure tenure should encourage farmers to invest in their land and access finance. We have not yet measured changes in disputes, investment or credit access; the 11 loans linked to titles are too few to analyse.

## Risks
Elite capture, chief buy-in, government platform integration, data privacy.

## Ask
USD 2.5m for 100,000 more parcels and a dispute-tracking system.
