# GuroLift — Seed Round

GuroLift is a mobile coaching app that gives primary-school teachers in public schools in Cebu and Leyte, Philippines, short video lessons and weekly peer-coaching prompts to improve early-grade reading instruction.

## The problem
Many Grade 2 learners cannot read at grade level, and most teachers receive little in-service coaching after training.

## Impact
- 2,300 teachers enrolled across 180 public schools; about 69,000 pupils taught by them.
- 71% of enrolled teachers completed at least 8 of 12 modules.
- Pupils' average oral reading fluency score rose from 31 to 46 words per minute between start and end of the school year (assessed by teachers using our rubric, before-and-after).
- Teacher survey: 84% say they use new reading routines weekly.

## Next steps
We have not yet run a comparison group study; we plan to partner with the Department of Education division office on a matched-schools evaluation.
