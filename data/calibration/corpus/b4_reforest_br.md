# Floresta Viva Carbon — Series A

Floresta Viva Carbon (fictional) develops reforestation projects in the Brazilian Amazon and Cerrado and sells carbon credits to corporate buyers seeking to become carbon neutral.

## Our story
We are nature positive. Our projects are restoring paradise, protecting biodiversity and delivering climate solutions at scale. Every credit is a tree, every tree is a promise.

## Portfolio
- 45,000 hectares claimed under restoration across six sites.
- Over 20 million trees planted or "in the pipeline".
- Credits sold to buyers in Europe and North America at USD 14-22 per tonne.
- Community benefits: local jobs, with partnerships with several villages.

## Carbon
We expect our forests to sequester very large volumes of CO2 for the long term. We use proprietary methods for our estimates; project registration with a standard is something we are exploring. Land is held under long leases and the forest is intended to be there for generations.

## Ask
USD 20m to acquire more land and expand sales to airlines and consumer brands.
