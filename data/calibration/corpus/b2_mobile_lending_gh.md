# Susu Bridge — Seed

Susu Bridge is a digital lender offering small working-capital loans (GHS 300–3,000) to market women traders in Accra, Kumasi and Tamale, Ghana, via USSD and a field agent network.

## Why we exist
Market traders borrow from informal lenders at 10%+ a week. Banks require collateral and formal records they do not have.

## Traction
- 18,500 active borrowers, 94% women, average loan GHS 850.
- Repayment rate of 91% on loans due (portfolio at risk 30 days: 6.2%).
- Our own client survey (n=640, October, phone-based): 72% said their business "grew" after borrowing, 58% reported higher weekly sales, and 64% said they stopped using informal lenders.

## Social approach
We cap effective interest at 4% per month, report to the credit bureau so borrowers build a history, and give every client financial literacy messages by SMS.

## Risks
Over-indebtedness; we limit borrowers to three consecutive loans without a savings check.
