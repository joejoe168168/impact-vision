# VerdeSuela — Pre-Series A

VerdeSuela (fictional) is a Valencia, Spain, sneaker brand selling a "climate positive" lifestyle collection online and in boutiques across Europe.

## Our mission
We are a planet-first, eco-conscious brand. Every pair is sustainably made with love for nature and a regenerative mindset. Our shoes are green, clean and good for the world.

## Why we are different
- Carbon neutral shoes, fully offset through our partner programme.
- Planet-friendly materials, including natural and eco fabrics.
- A portion of profits supports the environment.
- Our customers join a movement for a better tomorrow.

## Traction
Sales grew strongly in 2024 and our Instagram community passed 180k followers. Retail partners love the story.

## Vision
To make fashion that gives back more than it takes, becoming the leading net-positive footwear brand in Europe.

## Ask
EUR 6m for marketing, new stores and a wider range.
