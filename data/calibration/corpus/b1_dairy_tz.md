# Maziwa Bora Cooperative Network — Growth Round

Maziwa Bora builds solar-assisted milk chilling centres and runs collection routes for smallholder dairy farmers in Iringa, Mbeya and Njombe regions, Tanzania, selling chilled milk to processors in Dar es Salaam.

## The problem
Smallholders with two to five cows lose up to 20% of milk to spoilage in the hot hours before collection, and receive low farm-gate prices from informal traders.

## Traction
- 17 chilling centres operating; 5,200 registered farmers (about 38% women-headed households).
- 6.4 million litres collected in 2024, up from 3.1 million in 2023.
- Spoilage at centres below 3% (our own logs).
- We pay farmers on delivery through mobile money, usually within a week.

## Impact
We believe regular payment and a reliable buyer raise incomes and encourage investment in better cattle. We have not yet collected household income data and plan a baseline survey this year.
