# Vezelkring B.V. — Seed

Vezelkring collects post-consumer textiles from municipalities and retailers in the Netherlands and sorts them for reuse and recycling, with a pilot line that turns cotton-rich waste back into spinnable fibre.

## The problem
The Netherlands discards roughly 200,000 tonnes of textiles a year, and most recycled output is downcycled into insulation or rags.

## Traction
- 5,800 tonnes collected last year from 62 municipalities and 4 retail partners; 41% sorted for reuse, 35% downcycled, 24% sent to energy recovery.
- Our fibre-to-fibre line processed 420 tonnes, about 7% of intake; the output blends 20–30% recycled fibre into new yarn with a spinning partner.
- Life-cycle estimate prepared in-house: about 3.1 tCO2e avoided per tonne of fibre-to-fibre output versus virgin cotton (assumptions disclosed, not yet externally reviewed).
- 38 jobs, 14 of them for people far from the labour market via a sheltered-work partner.

## Risks
Contamination of mixed fibres lowers yield; the EU Extended Producer Responsibility rules should help demand.

## Ask
EUR 4 million to scale fibre-to-fibre capacity to 3,000 tonnes a year.
