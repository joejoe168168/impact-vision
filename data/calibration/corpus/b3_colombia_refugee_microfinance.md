# Puente Crédito — Series A

Puente Crédito provides small business loans and savings accounts to Venezuelan refugees and migrants living in Bogota, Cucuta and Medellin, Colombia, who lack the documentation required by banks.

## The problem
Millions of Venezuelans in Colombia are excluded from formal credit, leaving them reliant on informal lenders charging very high rates.

## Traction
- 14,600 loans disbursed since 2021, average size USD 380.
- 9,100 active borrowers; 68% women.
- Portfolio at risk over 30 days: 4.2%; repayment rate 96%.
- Interest rates average 31% annual, below the informal market.
- Net Promoter Score of 62 among borrowers.

## Impact
We help refugees build businesses and integrate into the Colombian economy. We plan to begin tracking income and employment outcomes next year.

## Ask
USD 7m of debt and equity to grow the loan book to 30,000 clients.
