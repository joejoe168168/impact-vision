# Alaga Nursing Institute — Series A

Alaga Institute operates a 12-month vocational nursing assistant programme for young people from low-income families in Cebu and Davao, Philippines, with clinical placements in partner hospitals and elder-care homes.

## The problem
Youth underemployment is high while hospitals and care homes in the Philippines and abroad struggle to fill care-worker roles.

## Results
- 2,350 graduates since 2019; 71% of intake from households under the poverty threshold.
- Completion rate 83%.
- Placement within six months of graduation: 87% (employer confirmation letters).
- Wage survey of 640 alumni, 12 months after graduation: median monthly income rose from PHP 6,800 before enrolment to PHP 15,200 now.
- 62% of graduates are women; 9% have migrated abroad for care work.

## What we do not yet know
We have no comparison group of similar applicants who did not enrol, so some wage growth may reflect age and general labour-market conditions.

## Ask
PHP 180m to open two new campuses and fund a tracer study with an external evaluator.
