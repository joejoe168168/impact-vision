# Sonrisa Justa — Series A

Sonrisa Justa operates 22 low-cost dental clinics in working-class neighbourhoods of Mexico City, Puebla and Guadalajara, Mexico, serving informal-sector workers without social-security dental coverage.

## The problem
Nearly 70% of Mexican adults report untreated tooth decay, and private clinics charge MXN 900-1,500 for a basic filling, out of reach for many households.

## Traction
- 214,000 patient visits in 2024 across 22 clinics; 88,000 unique patients, 61% first-time dental visitors in over three years (intake form).
- Average price of a filling MXN 380, versus MXN 1,100 at a sample of 40 private clinics we price-checked in the same neighbourhoods.
- 58% of patients earn under MXN 9,000 a month.
- Patient satisfaction score 4.6 / 5 (exit survey).

## Risks we manage
Clinical quality is audited quarterly by an external dentist. We do not yet track longer-term oral health outcomes.

## Ask
USD 7m to open 15 further clinics by 2027.
