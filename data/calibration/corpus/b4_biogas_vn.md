# BioCơ Digesters — Seed

BioCơ (fictional) installs low-cost biogas digesters on smallholder dairy farms in the Mekong Delta and Lam Dong, Vietnam, turning manure into cooking gas and slurry fertiliser.

## The problem
Smallholder dairy farmers dump manure into canals, producing methane and water pollution, while paying for LPG and chemical fertiliser.

## Traction
- 1,150 digesters installed since 2021, serving farms with 5 to 30 cows.
- Average farmer reports LPG spend down by about a third (phone survey of 300 customers, 2024).
- We estimate 4,800 tCO2e avoided per year using IPCC default factors for manure methane; not yet independently verified.
- 92% of digesters operational at 12 months based on our own technician visits.

## Business model
Farmers pay USD 420 over 24 months through a partner microfinance lender. Carbon revenue from a voluntary-market pilot is planned.

## Risks we manage
Digester abandonment after year three; lender default; methane leakage from poorly maintained units.

## Ask
USD 1.8m to reach 3,000 digesters and commission a verification of the emission estimate.
