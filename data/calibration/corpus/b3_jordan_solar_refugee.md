# NurCamp Energy — Seed

We install pay-as-you-go solar home systems in refugee camps and host-community settlements in Jordan, mainly around Zaatari and Azraq, in partnership with humanitarian agencies.

## The problem
Camp electricity is rationed and many households rely on kerosene lamps and diesel generators for the remaining hours.

## Traction
- 6,400 households equipped since 2022, roughly 29,000 people.
- 2.1 million kWh generated to date (inverter logs).
- Customer survey of 900 households: 78% say they stopped buying kerosene; average spend saving reported at JOD 11 per month.
- 84% of households say children study more in the evening.
- Repayment rate 91% on 24-month plans.

## Impact claims
We estimate about 1,900 tonnes CO2e avoided from kerosene and diesel displaced, using a standard emission factor and household-reported fuel use.

## Risks
Camp relocation and policy changes on who may sell energy products. Battery recycling agreement in place with a Jordanian recycler.

## Ask
USD 3.5m to reach 15,000 households by 2027.
