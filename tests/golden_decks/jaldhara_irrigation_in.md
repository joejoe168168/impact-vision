# Jaldhara Agritech — Seed Pitch

Jaldhara sells solar-powered drip irrigation kits on credit to smallholder farmers in Maharashtra and Karnataka, India.

## Traction
- 11,500 smallholder farmers equipped, average landholding 1.4 hectares; 31% of customers are women farmers.
- Farmers using our kits report 38% lower water use per acre and 24% higher yields versus their previous season (internal survey, n=1,200).
- 96% on-time repayment on kit financing.

## Risks we manage
- Over-indebtedness: instalments capped at 15% of expected seasonal income.
- Groundwater: we do not sell to farmers in over-exploited blocks listed by the Central Ground Water Board.

## Targets
50,000 farmers by 2027.
