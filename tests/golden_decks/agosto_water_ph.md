# Agosto Water Inc. — Seed Round

Agosto Water runs solar-powered water purification kiosks that sell safe drinking water to low-income households in Mindanao, Philippines.

## Traction
- 86 kiosks serving 54,000 people daily; 71% of customers live below the national poverty line.
- Water quality tested monthly by an accredited laboratory against Philippine National Standards for Drinking Water.
- Local health centres reported 22% fewer diarrhoea cases among children under five in served barangays (before-and-after comparison).

## Risks we manage
- Affordability: price capped at PHP 1 per litre.
- Waste: reject water is reused for community gardens; filter cartridges returned to the supplier.

## Targets
250 kiosks by 2028.
