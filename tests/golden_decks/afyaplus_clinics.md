# AfyaPlus Clinics — Series A Pitch

AfyaPlus runs a chain of 23 primary-care clinics in peri-urban Nairobi, Kisumu and Mombasa, Kenya, offering outpatient consultations, maternal and child health, diagnostics and a pharmacy.

## Impact so far
- 410,000 patient visits in 2024, up from 260,000 in 2023.
- 9,800 antenatal care visits and 2,100 facility deliveries in our 4 maternity units.
- Average consultation price KES 500 (about USD 4), 60% below private hospitals; 72% of patients are covered by SHA (Social Health Authority) or pay out of pocket under KES 1,000.
- 38% of patients earn less than USD 5 per day (patient intake survey, n=3,100).
- We employ 312 staff, including 140 nurses and 41 clinical officers; 66% women.
- Hypertension programme: 5,600 enrolled patients, 61% with controlled blood pressure at 6 months.

## Quality and safety
SafeCare level 3 accreditation for 15 clinics (PharmAccess). Medical waste handled by licensed incinerator contractor.

## Ask
USD 8m Series A to open 30 clinics and launch a telemedicine line.
