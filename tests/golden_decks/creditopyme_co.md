# CréditoPyme S.A.S. — Series A

CréditoPyme is a digital lender providing working-capital loans to micro and small businesses in Colombia, using transaction data from mobile payments for credit scoring.

## Reach
- 23,000 small businesses financed since 2021; 58% women-owned; 47% had no prior formal credit.
- Average loan size COP 4.2 million; portfolio at risk over 30 days 3.1%.
- A 2025 study by Universidad de los Andes found borrowers' revenues grew 19% more than a matched comparison group.

## Client protection
- Smart Campaign client-protection principles adopted; transparent pricing with total cost shown before signing.
- Complaints mechanism with 72-hour response target.

## Targets
75,000 small businesses by 2028.
