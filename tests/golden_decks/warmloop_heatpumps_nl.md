# WarmLoop B.V. — Series A

WarmLoop installs and finances air-to-water heat pumps for social-housing landlords in the Netherlands and Belgium, replacing gas boilers.

## Results to date
- 6,400 homes switched from gas boilers to heat pumps since 2022.
- Metered data across 2,100 homes show an average 62% cut in household heating emissions versus the 2021 baseline, verified by TNO.
- Average tenant energy bill down EUR 38 per month; 54% of tenants are on low incomes.

## Risks we manage
- Refrigerant leakage: all units use low-GWP R290; annual leak checks by certified technicians.
- End-of-life: take-back and recycling contract with the manufacturer.

## Targets
40,000 homes by 2028 and 95,000 tCO2e avoided per year.
