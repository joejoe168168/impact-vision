# Hearthstone Homes Ltd — Impact Fund Raise

Hearthstone Homes builds and manages affordable rental housing for key workers and low-income families in Manchester and Leeds, United Kingdom.

## Impact to date
- 1,240 affordable homes delivered, let at 65% of market rent; 38% of households were previously in temporary accommodation.
- All new homes meet EPC rating B or better; average tenant energy costs 30% below the local average.
- Tenant satisfaction 91% in an independent survey by Housemark.

## Risks we manage
- Evictions: no-fault evictions are not used; arrears support through a partnership with Citizens Advice.
- Building safety: fire-safety audits by accredited assessors every year.

## Targets
3,000 affordable homes by 2029.
