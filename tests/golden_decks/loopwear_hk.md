# LoopWear Limited — Pitch Deck

LoopWear is a Hong Kong circular-fashion company. We collect used garments through 85 drop-off bins in MTR stations and housing estates, sort them at our Kwai Chung facility, resell wearable items on our app, and send non-wearables to a fibre-to-fibre recycling partner.

## Traction (FY2024)
- 1,150 tonnes of textiles collected, of which 38% resold, 47% recycled into fibre, 15% sent to landfill or waste-to-energy.
- 52,000 app customers; average resale price HKD 80.
- Life-cycle assessment by a university team: each kg reused avoids about 11 kg CO2e versus new garments.
- 48 employees, including 12 people previously unemployed through an NGO partnership.

## Why it matters
Hong Kong sends around 340 tonnes of textiles to landfill every day (EPD Monitoring of Solid Waste 2022).

## Ask
HKD 25m Series A to open a second sorting hub and build B2B take-back for retailers.
