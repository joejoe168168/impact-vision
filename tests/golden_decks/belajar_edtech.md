# BelajarPintar — Seed Round Investment Memo

## Company
BelajarPintar (PT Belajar Pintar Nusantara) is an Android learning app and teacher dashboard for public junior secondary schools (SMP) in Indonesia, focused on Bahasa Indonesia literacy and mathematics.

## Problem
In Indonesia, PISA 2022 results show roughly 70% of 15-year-olds score below minimum proficiency in mathematics. Rural schools in East Nusa Tenggara and Papua have one teacher for 35+ students and few textbooks.

## Solution and traction
- 1,240 partner schools in 9 provinces; 186,000 monthly active students, 58% girls.
- 4,300 teachers trained on our adaptive-practice dashboard in 2024.
- Offline-first app works on $60 Android phones; content synced weekly.
- In a 2024 pilot with 40 schools in NTT run with a university partner, students using the app 3x a week gained 0.21 standard deviations in maths versus comparison schools.
- 64% of students are from households receiving the government PIP cash transfer.

## Business model
B2G: district education offices pay IDR 45,000 per student per year; free for students.

## Risks
Student data privacy: we comply with Indonesia's PDP Law and store data in-country. Screen time and dependency on district budgets.

## Ask
USD 3m seed to reach 1 million students by 2027.
