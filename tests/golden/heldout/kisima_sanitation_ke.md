# Kisima Sanitation Ltd — Seed Round

Kisima builds and services container-based toilets for informal settlements in Nairobi, Kenya, and turns the collected waste into briquettes.

## The problem
Around 60% of Nairobi residents live in informal settlements, and most share pit latrines.

## Results to date
- 2,400 household toilets installed, used by 11,500 people.
- 96% of waste collected weekly and treated, according to our service logs.
- 310 tonnes of briquettes sold in 2024, replacing charcoal.

## Risks we manage
- Worker safety: collectors wear full PPE and are vaccinated.

## Ask
USD 1.2m seed to reach 8,000 toilets by 2027.
