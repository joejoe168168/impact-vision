# Tujenge Homes — Growth Round

Tujenge builds affordable rent-to-own housing for low-income families in Kigali, Rwanda.

## Track record
- 640 homes delivered since 2021, housing 2,900 residents.
- Monthly payments average 28% of household income.
- 92% of tenants are on schedule to own their home within 15 years.

## Risks we manage
- Evictions: arrears support and a hardship fund before any eviction.
- Building safety: every home inspected by an accredited assessor.
