# Saigon Recycle Co. — Seed

Saigon Recycle collects plastic waste from households and informal waste pickers in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and sells recycled flakes to manufacturers.

## Traction
- 4,600 tonnes of plastic collected in 2024.
- 1,250 waste pickers sell to us at fair, published prices.
- 85% of collected plastic recycled; the rest goes to licensed waste-to-energy.
