# MediSync Health — Series A

MediSync runs a telemedicine and pharmacy-delivery service for patients with chronic diseases in Lagos and Abuja, Nigeria.

## Traction
- 42,000 patients registered; 18,500 active monthly.
- 64% of hypertensive patients had their blood pressure under control after six months (clinic records, before and after).
- Average consultation cost of NGN 2,500, about a third of a private clinic visit.

## Risks we manage
- Patient data is encrypted and stored under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
