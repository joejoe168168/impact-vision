# Aprende+ — Seed

Aprende+ offers vocational coding bootcamps for young people from low-income neighbourhoods of Medellín, Colombia.

## Outcomes
- 1,900 students graduated since 2022.
- 74% of graduates found formal jobs within six months.
- Graduates' average monthly income rose from COP 1.1 million to COP 2.6 million.
