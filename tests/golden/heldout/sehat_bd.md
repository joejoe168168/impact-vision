# Sehat Clinics — Series A

Sehat runs nurse-led primary-care clinics in garment-factory districts of Dhaka, Bangladesh.

## Impact
- 210,000 patient visits in 2024 across 24 clinics.
- 58% of patients are women garment workers.
- Antenatal visits rose to 4.1 per pregnancy, from 1.8 before our clinics opened (district health data).
