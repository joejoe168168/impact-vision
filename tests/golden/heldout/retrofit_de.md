# WärmeWerk GmbH — Series A

WärmeWerk plans and installs deep energy retrofits with heat pumps for apartment buildings owned by housing cooperatives in Germany.

## Results
- 2,350 apartments retrofitted since 2022.
- Heating energy use fell 58% on average (metered data, before and after).
- 6,900 tCO2e avoided per year.
