# Creditar — Series A

Creditar is a digital lender offering working-capital loans to micro-entrepreneurs in the favelas of São Paulo, Brazil.

## Portfolio
- 33,000 micro-entrepreneurs served, 68% of them women.
- BRL 120 million disbursed since 2022.
- 72% of repeat clients report higher monthly sales (client survey).
