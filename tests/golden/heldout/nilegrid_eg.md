# NileGrid Energy — Series B

NileGrid builds solar mini-grids for villages in Upper Egypt that the national grid does not reach reliably.

## Results
- 38 mini-grids connecting 21,000 households.
- 31,000 tCO2e avoided in 2024, verified by an independent auditor under the Gold Standard.
- In an evaluation with the American University in Cairo, household energy spending fell 34% versus a matched comparison group of villages.

## Risks we manage
- Battery end-of-life: all batteries go to a licensed recycler under a take-back contract.
