# StitchRight Apparel — Growth

StitchRight is a garment manufacturer in Chattogram, Bangladesh, supplying European fashion brands.

## Workforce
- 3,800 workers, 72% of them women.
- All workers are paid a living wage, certified by the Fair Wear Foundation.
- Lost-time injury rate fell from 2.4 to 0.6 per million hours since 2021.
