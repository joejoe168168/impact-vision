# CareLoop Ltd — Seed

CareLoop provides home care for older people in the North East of England, with carers on salaried contracts.

## Impact
- 1,150 older people receive care at home each week.
- Carer turnover is 18% a year against a sector average of 30%.
- Hospital readmissions within 30 days were 11% for our clients in 2024.

## Risks we manage
- Safeguarding: every carer is DBS-checked and trained in safeguarding.
