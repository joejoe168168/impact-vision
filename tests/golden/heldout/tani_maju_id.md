# Tani Maju — Series A

Tani Maju is an agri-marketplace that links smallholder rice farmers in Java, Indonesia, to millers and sells inputs on credit.

## Results
- 27,000 smallholder farmers active on the platform.
- Farmers received 18% higher farm-gate prices than the district average in 2024.
- IDR 410 billion of harvest traded.
