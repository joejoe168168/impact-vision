# SokoFresh Ghana — Seed

SokoFresh rents solar-powered cold rooms to smallholder farmers near markets in the Ashanti region of Ghana.

## Impact so far
- 3,100 smallholder farmers used our cold rooms in 2024.
- Post-harvest losses for tomatoes fell from 40% to 12% (our monitoring data across 9 sites).
- Farmer incomes rose 28% on average, from a survey of 400 farmers before and after joining.
