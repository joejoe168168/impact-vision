# Bayani Insurance — Seed

Bayani sells mobile micro-insurance for typhoon damage to low-income households and sari-sari stores in the Visayas, Philippines.

## Traction
- 76,000 policyholders, 61% of them first-time insurance buyers.
- 5,300 claims paid in 2024, 90% within 72 hours.
- Premiums start at PHP 50 a month.
