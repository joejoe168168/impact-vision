# Ubuntu Clinics — Series A

Ubuntu Clinics runs low-cost GP and chronic-care clinics in townships around Johannesburg, South Africa.

## Results
- 96,000 patient visits in 2024 across 11 clinics.
- 81% of patients with HIV on treatment are virally suppressed (clinic records).
- A consultation costs ZAR 250, a fifth of a private GP visit.
