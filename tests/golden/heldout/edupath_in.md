# EduPath Learning — Series A

EduPath provides an adaptive maths app and teacher coaching for government primary schools in Uttar Pradesh, India.

## Evidence
- 120,000 students in 1,450 schools used EduPath in 2024.
- A randomised controlled trial run by J-PAL found test scores rose 0.3 standard deviations versus control schools.
- Results externally verified by an independent evaluator.

## Ask
USD 10m to reach 1 million students.
