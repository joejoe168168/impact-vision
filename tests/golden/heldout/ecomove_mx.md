# EcoMove Transit — Series A

EcoMove leases electric buses to public transport operators in Guadalajara and Monterrey, Mexico.

## Operations
- 140 electric buses in service, carrying 9 million passengers in 2024.
- 11,800 tCO2e avoided in 2024 compared with diesel buses on the same routes.
- 320 drivers trained and employed by partner operators.

## Risks we manage
- Road safety: driver training and telematics on every bus.
