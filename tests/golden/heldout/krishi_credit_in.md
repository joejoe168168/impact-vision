# Krishi Credit — Pre-Series A

Krishi Credit gives small working-capital loans to women-led dairy cooperatives in Rajasthan, India.

## Portfolio
- 9,800 borrowers, 87% of them women.
- 14,200 loans disbursed since 2022 with an average size of INR 35,000.
- Portfolio at risk over 30 days is 2.1%.

## Client protection
- We check repayment capacity and cap loans at 30% of monthly income.

USD 4m equity.
